### Hi, I'm Bipin 👋

Software engineer building systems in the retail space — frontend, backend, cloud, and infrastructure. Mostly .NET and JavaScript, with a lot of Azure and Terraform underneath.

Lately I spend less time hand-writing code and more time designing systems, specs, and workflows around AI-assisted development and agentic systems. "Writing software" quietly evolved into "orchestrating extremely confident robots."

I care about readable code, good observability, and systems that are understandable by humans other than their original author six months later.

**Outside code:** thrillers, sci-fi, good coffee, and the occasional rabbit hole at unreasonable hours.
